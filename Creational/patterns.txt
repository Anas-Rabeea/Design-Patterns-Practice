# No Business Logic in the Factory - Factory creates Objects ONLY
Simple Factory   : Need to centralize object creation - many new Payment() - Long if-else
Factory Method   : Need subclasses to decide what gets created?
Abstract Factory : Need families of related objects?


With Simple Factory, there is still one central class making the decision.
With Factory Method, the decision is distributed into subclasses.
That architectural shift is the entire reason Factory Method exists
